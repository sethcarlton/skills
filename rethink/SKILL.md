---
name: rethink
description: Rethink the current work from first principles before calling it done.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Think from first principles about what we're trying to achieve. Before calling the work done, ask:

1. Is anything needless, too complex, or based on weak assumptions? Check it against the goal and constraints.
2. What can you delete?
3. What can you simplify once those pieces are gone?

Make the changes. Prefer deleting over simplifying, simplifying over optimizing, and optimizing over automating. Preserve required behavior and verify your changes.

Don't change things just to change them. If the work is good, leave it alone.

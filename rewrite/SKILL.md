Rewrite your immediately previous response as polished prose suitable for docs, PR text, or messages. Preserve its meaning and important caveats.

1. Never use a metaphor, simile, or other figure of speech that you commonly see in print.
2. Never use a long word when a short one will do.
3. If you can cut a word, always cut it.
4. Never use passive voice when you can use active voice.
5. Never use a foreign phrase, scientific term, or jargon if you can think of an everyday English equivalent.
6. Break any of these rules rather than write something outright ugly.
